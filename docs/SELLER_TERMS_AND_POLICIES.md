# OrignaGTA Seller Terms and Policies

*Last Updated: February 3, 2026*

## 1. Payment Processing & Payout Terms

### 1.1 Payment Providers

OrignaGTA supports two payment providers for seller payouts:

#### Stripe (Recommended for Canadian Sellers)
- **Payout Timing**: 7 days after delivery confirmation
- **Transaction Fees**: 2.9% + $0.30 CAD per transaction
- **Platform Fee**: 2.5% commission on each sale
- **Currencies**: CAD (primary), USD
- **Setup**: Instant via Stripe Express
- **Best For**: Canadian-based sellers with domestic inventory

#### Airwallex (Recommended for International Sellers)
- **Payout Timing**: Weekly batch every Friday at 4:00 PM EST
- **Total Processing Time**: 7-14 business days
- **Transaction Fees**: Competitive rates, varies by volume
- **Platform Fee**: 2.5% commission on each sale (negotiable for high volume)
- **Currencies**: CAD, USD, CNY, EUR, and 40+ currencies
- **Best For**: International sellers, dropshipping businesses, multi-currency needs

### 1.2 Payout Hold Period

All payouts are subject to a **7-day hold period** after delivery confirmation to:
- Allow buyers time to inspect products
- Process any disputes or returns
- Ensure order fulfillment quality

The hold period may be adjusted for:
- **Verified sellers**: Reduced to 3-5 days after 50+ successful orders
- **High-risk transactions**: Extended to 14 days if flagged by fraud detection

### 1.3 Commission Structure

| Seller Tier | Monthly Sales | Commission Rate |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Standard | $0 - $5,000 | 2.5% |
| Silver | $5,001 - $20,000 | 2.25% |
| Gold | $20,001 - $50,000 | 2.0% |
| Platinum | $50,001+ | Negotiable (contact sales) |

## 2. Shipping & Fulfillment Policies

### 2.1 Domestic Shipping (Canada)

Sellers shipping from Canadian locations must:
- Ship within 3 business days of order confirmation
- Provide tracking numbers for all orders over $30
- Use carriers with delivery confirmation
- Package items securely to prevent damage

**Shipping Cost Guidelines:**
| Distance | Standard Rate | Express Rate |
|----------|---------------|--------------|
| Local (0-50 km) | $1.99 - $4.99 | $7.99 |
| Regional (50-500 km) | $9.99 - $14.99 | $14.99 - $19.99 |
| National (500+ km) | $18.99 - $26.99 | $29.99 - $39.99 |

### 2.2 International Suppliers (Dropshipping)

Sellers using international suppliers (AliExpress, DHgate, Alibaba, etc.) must:

1. **Disclose Shipping Times**: Clearly state expected delivery windows
2. **Set Accurate Expectations**: 
   - AliExpress Standard: 15-30 business days
   - DHgate Standard: 20-40 business days
   - Temu/CJ Dropshipping: 7-15 business days
   - Express options: 7-15 business days (with surcharge)

3. **Track Orders**: Provide supplier tracking numbers when available
4. **Handle Customs**: Inform buyers about potential customs fees
5. **Quality Control**: Be responsible for supplier product quality

**Supplier Type Shipping Estimates:**

| Supplier | Standard Delivery | Express Delivery |
|----------|-------------------|------------------|
| AliExpress | 15-30 days | 7-15 days |
| DHgate | 20-40 days | 10-20 days |
| Alibaba | 25-45 days | 7-15 days |
| 1688 | 30-50 days | 10-20 days |
| Temu | 7-15 days | 5-10 days |
| CJ Dropshipping | 10-20 days | 5-10 days |

### 2.3 Perishable & Local-Only Items

Products marked as "perishable" or "local delivery only":
- Must be delivered within **50 km** of seller location (`LOCAL_DELIVERY_RADIUS_KM = 50.0`)
- Cannot be shipped outside the seller's province
- Require same-day or next-day delivery options (platform enforces this — products without these options are auto-deactivated per CFIA compliance)
- Must include proper temperature control packaging
- Cross-province perishable shipments incur an additional surcharge
- Buyers outside the 50 km radius cannot purchase local-only items

## 3. Product Listing Requirements

### 3.1 Required Information

All product listings must include:
- Clear, accurate product title (2-120 characters)
- Detailed description (minimum 10 characters)
- At least 1 product image (maximum 5)
- Accurate pricing in CAD
- Current stock quantity
- Category classification
- Seller's shipping address/origin

### 3.2 Optional but Recommended

For better buyer experience and sales:
- Product dimensions (L x W x H in cm)
- Weight in kg
- Tax code (for accurate tax calculation)
- Multiple delivery options
- Supplier information (for transparency)
- Keywords for search optimization

### 3.3 Supplier Information (Dropshipping)

If sourcing from suppliers, include in your internal records:
- Supplier platform type (AliExpress, DHgate, etc.)
- Supplier SKU/Product ID
- Cost price (for margin tracking)
- Supplier URL
- Expected shipping days
- Tracking availability

## 4. Inventory Management

### 4.1 Stock Tracking Options

| Mode | Description | Best For |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Managed + Tracked** | Full quantity tracking with alerts | Standard sellers with inventory |
| **Managed + Unlimited** | No quantity limits | Digital products, high-stock items |
| **Unmanaged** | No automatic stock deduction | Dropshipping, made-to-order |

### 4.2 Backorder Policy

Sellers may enable backorders if:
- They can reliably fulfill within stated timeframe
- Product is clearly marked as "backorder"
- Buyer is informed before purchase
- Maximum backorder wait time: 30 days

### 4.3 Low Stock Alerts

Configure alerts when stock falls below threshold (default: 5 units).
This helps prevent overselling and disappointed customers.

---

## 5. Returns & Refunds

### 5.1 Standard Return Policy

- **Return Window**: 14 days from delivery
- **Condition**: Item must be unused and in original packaging
- **Refund Processing**: 3-5 business days after item received

### 5.2 Dropshipping Returns

For products sourced from international suppliers:
- Seller is responsible for return coordination
- Buyer ships to seller's Canadian address (not to supplier)
- Refund issued within 5 business days of inspection
- Seller absorbs return shipping if item is defective

### 5.3 Non-Returnable Items

- Perishable goods
- Personal hygiene products
- Custom/personalized items
- Digital products (after download)
- Items marked "Final Sale"

## 6. Seller Verification

### 6.1 Verification Levels

| Level | Requirements | Benefits |
|-------|--------------|----------|
| **Basic** | Email + Phone verified | Can list products |
| **Verified** | ID + Address verified | Lower hold period, higher limits |
| **Premium** | Business registration + Tax ID | Priority support, custom rates |

### 6.2 Required Documents

For full verification, sellers must provide:
- Government-issued ID (passport, driver's license)
- Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement)
- Business registration (if applicable)
- Tax registration number (GST/HST if over $30,000/year)

## 7. Prohibited Activities

Sellers must NOT:
- List counterfeit or trademark-infringing products
- Misrepresent product origin, quality, or specifications
- Manipulate reviews or ratings
- Engage in price gouging during emergencies
- Sell prohibited items (weapons, drugs, etc.)
- Use multiple accounts to circumvent restrictions
- Share customer data with third parties

Violations result in:
- First offense: Warning + 7-day suspension
- Second offense: 30-day suspension + payout hold
- Third offense: Permanent account termination

---

## 8. Dispute Resolution

### 8.1 Buyer Disputes

1. Buyer initiates dispute within 14 days of delivery
2. Seller has 48 hours to respond
3. If unresolved, OrignaGTA mediates
4. Final decision within 7 business days

### 8.2 Seller Protections

Sellers are protected if:
- Tracking shows successful delivery
- Product matches listing description
- All platform policies were followed

### 8.3 Chargeback Protection

OrignaGTA provides limited chargeback protection for verified sellers:
- Coverage up to $500 per transaction
- Maximum $2,000 per month
- Requires full documentation of shipment

## 9. Tax Compliance

### 9.1 Canadian Tax Obligations

Sellers are responsible for:
- Collecting and remitting GST/HST if required
- Registering for GST/HST when exceeding $30,000/year
- Maintaining accurate sales records
- Filing appropriate tax returns

### 9.2 International Sellers

Non-Canadian sellers must:
- Understand Canadian import duties
- Provide accurate customs declarations
- Not misrepresent product values
- Comply with Canadian consumer protection laws
