# International Shipping Terms for Sellers

## Customs, Duties, and Import Taxes

### Seller Responsibility

When selling products sourced from international suppliers (e.g., AliExpress, DHGate, Alibaba, Temu, CJ Dropshipping), **the seller is solely responsible for ensuring all customs duties, import taxes, and brokerage fees are included in the product price**.

### Requirements

1. **Price Inclusivity**: Product prices listed on OrignaGTA must include:
   - All Canadian customs duties (based on HS code classification)
   - GST/HST on imported goods
   - Any brokerage or handling fees
   - Standard shipping costs

2. **No Surprise Charges**: Buyers must never be asked to pay additional fees upon delivery.

3. **Clear Disclosure**: Product listings must indicate if items ship from outside Canada.

### Import Duty Estimates (Reference)

| Product Category | Estimated Duty Rate | HS Code Examples |
|-----------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Clothing/Textiles | 18-25% | 61xx, 62xx |
| Electronics | 0-8% | 8517, 8528 |
| Toys/Games | 8-10% | 9503, 9504 |
| Jewelry | 8-15% | 7113, 7116 |
| Home Goods | 5-15% | varies |

### Best Practices

1. **Source from Canadian Warehouses** when possible to eliminate customs delays
2. **Use Delivered Duty Paid (DDP)** shipping options from suppliers
3. **Build in Buffer**: Add 20-25% to product cost for international items to cover duty uncertainty
4. **Track Shipments**: Provide tracking numbers to buyers for cross-border orders

### Delivery Timelines

International shipments typically take:
- **Standard shipping**: 15-40 business days
- **Express shipping**: 7-15 business days

Buyers must be informed of extended delivery times for international products.

### Compliance

Failure to comply with these terms may result in:
- Account suspension
- Financial liability for unexpected buyer charges
- Removal from the platform
